# Sand Hill VC depth pass

This batch deepens the existing semiconductor VC world rather than adding another shallow game.

## Player loop

Source founders → diligence → investment committee → portfolio financing / board events → liquidity / exits.

## Fund mechanics

- Track TVPI and DPI separately instead of one generic MOIC number.
- Preserve ownership by using follow-on reserves during financing rounds, or accept dilution by sitting out.
- Use operating budget for board-level support after design wins or customer slips; do not conflate operating support with investment reserves.
- Portfolio events include up rounds, bridge rounds, down rounds, design wins, and customer qualification slips.
- Realize sufficiently marked-up holdings at the liquidity desk, converting unrealized NAV into distributions and DPI.
- Keep dry powder, reserves spent, distributions, ownership, staff, reputation, and operating budget visible as separate resources.

## Identity

Sand Hill VC remains an isometric venture-office management game. It should not inherit Market Maker's execution-routing loop or Hedge Fund HQ's public-markets research/long-short loop. The unique verbs are sourcing, diligence, ownership decisions, reserve management, board support, and exits.
